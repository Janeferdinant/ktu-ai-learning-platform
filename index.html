📋 APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University Syllabus

Official 44-contact-hour course structure, Course Outcomes (CO1–CO4), Bloom's Taxonomy, and evaluation mapping.

44
Contact Hours
4
Credits (3:1:0)
Module 1: Foundations of AI & Machine Learning
11 Hours • CO1

Bloom Taxonomy: Levels 1, 2, 3 (Remember, Understand, Apply)

  • Introduction to Artificial Intelligence: Definitions, Turing Test, State Space Search, Heuristic Search ($A^*$ Algorithm).
  • Machine Learning paradigms: Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning.
  • Single-Layer Perceptron: Architecture, Linear Separability, Perceptron Learning Rule, Proof of Convergence.
  • Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP): Activation functions (Sigmoid, ReLU, Tanh), Forward pass, Backpropagation gradient derivation.
  • Loss functions: Mean Squared Error (MSE), Binary Cross-Entropy.
Module 2: Linear Algebra, SVD & PCA
11 Hours • CO2

Bloom Taxonomy: Levels 2, 3, 4 (Understand, Apply, Analyze)

  • Vector Spaces, Linear Independence, Basis, Rank of a Matrix, Inner Products and Norms.
  • Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors: Characteristic equation $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$, Geometric interpretation.
  • Singular Value Decomposition (SVD): $X = U \Sigma V^T$, Left/Right Singular vectors, Singular values.
  • Principal Component Analysis (PCA): Covariance matrix $\Sigma$, Maximizing variance, Dimensionality reduction, Low-rank matrix approximation.
Module 3: Probability, Bayes & Continuous Distributions
11 Hours • CO3

Bloom Taxonomy: Levels 2, 3 (Understand, Apply)

  • Random variables, Probability mass/density functions, Expectation and Variance.
  • Joint, Marginal, and Conditional probability: Chain Rule, Law of Total Probability.
  • Bayes' Theorem: Prior, Likelihood, Evidence, Posterior. Naive Bayes Classifier and Conditional Independence Assumption.
  • Continuous Distributions: Gaussian (Normal) Distribution, Central Limit Theorem (CLT), Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE).
Module 4: Supervised, Unsupervised Learning & Real-World AI
11 Hours • CO4

Bloom Taxonomy: Levels 2, 3, 4 (Understand, Apply, Evaluate)

  • Linear Regression: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) closed-form solution and Gradient Descent.
  • Logistic Regression: Sigmoid transformation, Log-Odds (Logit), Decision Boundary.
  • K-Means Clustering: Algorithm steps, Lloyd's heuristic, Voronoi partitioning, Elbow method.
  • Model Evaluation: Confusion Matrix, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC Curve, AUC, Bias-Variance Tradeoff.
